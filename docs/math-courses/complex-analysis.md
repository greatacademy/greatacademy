# Complex Analysis

> This is Great Academy's own outline, not from the reference catalog. Basis: the standard content of a first course, as in Churchill and Brown or Ahlfors. [简体中文](complex-analysis.zh-CN.md) · [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** University
- **Prerequisite(s):** Multivariable Calculus
- **Size:** 4 units · 7 modules · 59 topics

## Description

Calculus for functions of a complex variable: analytic functions and the Cauchy–Riemann equations, contour integrals and Cauchy's theorems, Taylor and Laurent series, residues and their applications, analytic continuation, conformal maps, and the Dirichlet problem.

## Outcomes

- Differentiate complex functions, and use the Cauchy-Riemann equations and harmonic conjugates.
- Compute contour integrals and apply Cauchy's theorem and integral formula.
- Expand functions in Taylor and Laurent series, and classify singularities.
- Evaluate integrals and sums with residues, and count zeros with the argument principle.
- Build conformal maps, including Möbius and Schwarz-Christoffel maps, and state the Riemann mapping theorem.
- Use normal families and expansions of meromorphic functions in terms of their poles.

## Scope decisions

- Riemann surfaces and the full Weierstrass and Hadamard factorization theorems belong to a second course; the product formula for sine stands in for them.
