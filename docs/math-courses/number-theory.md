# Number Theory

> This is Great Academy's own outline, not from the reference catalog. Basis: the standard content of a first undergraduate course, as in Niven, Zuckerman and Montgomery or Hardy and Wright. [简体中文](number-theory.zh-CN.md) · [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** University
- **Prerequisite(s):** Methods of Proof
- **Size:** 5 units · 10 modules · 78 topics

## Description

Divisors and primes, powers modulo n through the theorems of Fermat and Euler, primitive roots and discrete logarithms, quadratic residues and reciprocity, arithmetic functions and the size of the primes, classic equations in whole numbers, and factoring and public-key cryptography.

## Outcomes

- Use divisibility, the Euclidean algorithm and unique factorization.
- Solve congruences and use the theorems of Fermat, Euler, Wilson and the Chinese remainder theorem.
- Work with orders, primitive roots, quadratic residues and quadratic reciprocity.
- Use arithmetic functions, Möbius inversion and Dirichlet series.
- Solve Diophantine equations with descent, quadratic forms, continued fractions and Pell's equation.
- Apply number theory to primality, factoring and public-key cryptography.
- Meet p-adic numbers and the group of an elliptic curve modulo a prime.

## Scope decisions

- Proofs of the prime number theorem and of Hasse's theorem are stated, not derived; both need analysis beyond this course.
