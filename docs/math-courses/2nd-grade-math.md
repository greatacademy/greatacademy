# 2nd Grade Math

> This is Great Academy's own outline, not from the reference catalog. Basis: the Common Core State Standards for this grade. [简体中文](2nd-grade-math.zh-CN.md) · [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** Elementary School
- **Prerequisite(s):** 1st Grade Math
- **Size:** 14 units · 22 modules · 65 topics

## Description

Place value to 1000, adding and subtracting within 100 and 1000, word problems, measuring length, time, money, graphs and shapes.

## Outcomes

- Add and subtract within 100 in one- and two-step stories, and know sums within 20 from memory (2.OA).
- Work with even and odd numbers and with arrays, as a foundation for multiplication (2.OA).
- Understand place value to 1,000, and add and subtract within 1,000 (2.NBT).
- Measure and estimate lengths, tell time to five minutes, count money, and draw picture graphs, bar graphs and line plots (2.MD).
- Recognize shapes by their angles and faces, and partition rectangles and circles into equal shares (2.G).
