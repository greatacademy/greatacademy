# 3rd Grade Math

> This is Great Academy's own outline, not from the reference catalog. Basis: the Common Core State Standards for this grade. [简体中文](3rd-grade-math.zh-CN.md) · [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** Elementary School
- **Prerequisite(s):** 2nd Grade Math
- **Size:** 14 units · 26 modules · 65 topics

## Description

The meaning of multiplication and division, the facts up to 10 times 10, and the properties that make them easier; two-step word problems and number patterns; rounding and three-digit addition and subtraction; fractions as numbers on a number line; time to the minute, mass and liquid volume; scaled graphs and line plots; area, perimeter and the sorting of quadrilaterals.

## Outcomes

- Understand multiplication and division, know the facts to 100, and solve two-step problems (3.OA).
- Round to tens and hundreds, and add and subtract fluently within 1,000 (3.NBT).
- Understand fractions as numbers on a number line, and find and compare equivalent fractions (3.NF).
- Tell elapsed time, measure mass and volume, read scaled graphs, and find area and perimeter (3.MD).
- Reason about categories of shapes, and cut shapes into parts of equal area (3.G).
