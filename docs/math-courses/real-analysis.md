# Real Analysis

> This is Great Academy's own outline, not from the reference catalog. Basis: the standard content of a first course, as in Abbott or the first eleven chapters of Rudin's Principles. [简体中文](real-analysis.zh-CN.md) · [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** University
- **Prerequisite(s):** Calculus II and Methods of Proof
- **Size:** 5 units · 8 modules · 82 topics

## Description

The rigorous foundations of calculus: completeness, sequences and series, limits and continuity through epsilons and deltas, compactness and connectedness, the Riemann and Stieltjes integrals, a first look at measure, and uniform convergence with its applications.

## Outcomes

- Prove limits of sequences and series from the definitions, and use completeness of the real numbers.
- Work with open, closed, compact and connected sets in the line and in metric spaces.
- Prove the main theorems about continuity, the derivative and the Riemann integral.
- Decide when limits may be exchanged, using uniform convergence.
- Use approximation theorems, equicontinuity, the contraction principle and Fourier series.
- Understand Lebesgue measure and integrals, with the monotone and dominated convergence theorems.

## Scope decisions

- The inverse function theorem is taught in Multivariable Calculus and is not repeated here. L^p spaces belong to a second course.
