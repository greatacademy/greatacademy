# 6th Grade Math

> Source: <https://www.mathacademy.com/courses/6th-grade-math> (retrieved 2026-10-30). Part of the [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** Middle School
- **Prerequisite(s):** 5th Grade Math
- **Size:** 8 units · 28 modules · 157 topics

## Description

This Common Core-aligned 6th Grade Math course builds on the foundations established in the 5th Grade course and expands students’ understanding of number, operations, algebraic thinking, geometry, and data analysis. Students deepen their fluency with ratios, rates, percentages, multi-digit division, fraction division, decimals, rational numbers, expressions, equations, geometry, and statistics.

Throughout the course, students learn to represent quantitative relationships in tables, graphs, diagrams, and equations; solve real-world problems involving proportional reasoning, unit rates, percentages, and measurement conversions; and reason flexibly about numerical and algebraic structure. They also develop a stronger understanding of negative numbers, the coordinate plane, exponents, geometric measurement, and statistical summaries and displays.

## Overview

Students begin by studying ratios, equivalent ratios, ratio tables, tape diagrams, unit rates, percentages, and unit conversions. They then extend their fluency with whole number division, fraction division, and decimal operations, while strengthening their understanding of place value and the meaning of division in increasingly complex settings.

The course next introduces rational numbers, including negative numbers, absolute value, ordering rational numbers, coordinate plane reasoning, and exponents. Students then transition into expressions and equations, where they learn to write, evaluate, simplify, and factor algebraic expressions; solve one-step equations; represent inequalities; and analyze two-variable relationships using tables and graphs.

In geometry, students investigate area, perimeter, surface area, volume, symmetry, and coordinate geometry. The course concludes with statistics and probability, where students summarize distributions using measures of center and spread, interpret outliers, and represent data using frequency tables, dot plots, box plots, and histograms.

## Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will have mastered the following: I. Ratios & Proportional Relationships

- Understand ratios and write, compare, approximate, and reason with equivalent ratios.
- Represent ratio relationships using ratio tables, graphs, and tape diagrams.
- Solve problems involving unit rates, including speed.
- Convert among fractions, decimals, and percentages, and compare values across these forms.
- Solve percentage problems involving parts, wholes, and percents.
- Convert measurement units within metric and customary systems, including units of length, volume, mass, and time.
- Divide multi-digit whole numbers by one-digit and two-digit divisors.
- Interpret remainders appropriately in context.
- Estimate quotients and connect division to multiplication.
- Use fractions to represent division of whole numbers.
- Divide whole numbers by fractions and fractions by whole numbers using models and procedures.
- Understand and apply reciprocals when dividing fractions.
- Divide fractions by fractions and interpret fraction division in real-world contexts.
- Divide mixed numbers in a variety of forms and solve word problems involving fraction division.
- Add and subtract decimals, including numbers with unequal decimal places.
- Multiply decimals by whole numbers and by other decimals.
- Divide whole numbers and decimals to obtain decimal quotients.
- Estimate decimal quotients and apply decimal operations in multi-step settings.
- Understand negative numbers, absolute value, and the role of rational numbers in the number system.
- Compare and order rational numbers.
- Represent and reason about rational numbers on the coordinate plane.
- Reflect points and solve problems in the coordinate plane.
- Evaluate expressions with exponents, including those with fractional and decimal bases.
- Write, construct, and evaluate algebraic expressions.
- Identify terms, coefficients, constants, and like terms.
- Simplify expressions, apply the distributive law, factor expressions, and identify equivalent expressions.
- Solve one-step linear equations, including equations involving fractions.
- Model and solve real-world problems using equations and inequalities.
- Represent and analyze two-variable equations using tables and graphs.
- Find areas of rectangles, triangles, parallelograms, and other polygons.
- Solve problems involving perimeter, area, and composite regions.
- Recognize lines of symmetry.
- Identify three-dimensional figures and describe their faces, edges, and vertices.
- Use nets to reason about surface area.
- Find volumes of right rectangular prisms, including prisms with fractional edge lengths.
- Solve geometric problems in the coordinate plane involving distance, perimeter, and area.
- Summarize data using mean, median, mode, range, quartiles, interquartile range, and mean absolute deviation.
- Identify and interpret outliers and reason about how outliers affect data summaries.
- Compare measures of center and spread across data sets.
- Represent data using frequency tables, dot plots, box plots, and histograms.
- Interpret distribution shape, including symmetry and skew, and connect visual displays to statistical measures.
