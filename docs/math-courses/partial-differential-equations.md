# Partial Differential Equations

> This is Great Academy's own outline, not from the reference catalog. Basis: the standard content of a first course, as in Strauss or Haberman. [简体中文](partial-differential-equations.zh-CN.md) · [math curriculum catalog](../math-curriculum.md).

- **Level:** University
- **Prerequisite(s):** Differential Equations and Multivariable Calculus
- **Size:** 4 units · 6 modules · 56 topics

## Description

Equations in several variables: classification, characteristics and shocks, separation of variables and Sturm-Liouville problems, the heat, wave and Laplace equations, transforms, Green's functions, special functions, and finite differences with their stability.

## Outcomes

- Classify second-order equations and solve first-order equations by characteristics.
- Solve boundary value problems by separation of variables and Sturm-Liouville expansions.
- Solve the heat, wave and Laplace equations, with transforms on unbounded regions.
- Use maximum principles, energy methods, Green's functions and fundamental solutions.
- Understand weak solutions and distributions.
- Discretize equations with finite differences and finite elements, and judge stability.

## Scope decisions

- Sobolev spaces and existence theory for weak solutions belong to a graduate course.
