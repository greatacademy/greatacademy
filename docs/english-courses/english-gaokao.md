# Gaokao English

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §6.2. [简体中文](english-gaokao.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `english-gaokao`, in the group English Exams
- **Level:** CEFR B1 and above, for students preparing for the English paper of China's national college entrance examination
- **Prerequisite(s):** English B1 (`english-b1`)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 3 units · 47 topics (6 new, all practice-only) · 1 reading unit of 8 passages

## The Test

Checked on 2026-11-10 against the National Education Examinations Authority's description of the new-curriculum paper, [neea.edu.cn: English new-curriculum paper of the 2024 adaptive test, with experts' reading](https://www.neea.edu.cn/html1/report/2401/499-1.htm), and the Ministry of Education's [notes on the English paper](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/s5147/202007/t20200709_470940.html). The numbers of questions are those of the papers set on that description.

| Part | Format | In this course |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Listening | 20 multiple-choice questions on recorded conversations and talks (30 points) | Not yet: see below |
| Reading (50 points) | Reading comprehension: four texts with 15 multiple-choice questions; seven-from-five: a text with five gaps and seven sentences to choose from | Unit 1 |
| Language use (30 points) | Cloze: a text with 15 gaps of four options (15 points); grammar filling: a text of about 200 words with 10 gaps, some with a word to change (15 points) | Unit 2 |
| Writing (40 points) | Practical writing: a short email, notice or similar text of about 80 words for a situation (15 points); continuation writing: a story of under 350 words continued in two paragraphs of about 150 words in all, each beginning with a given sentence (25 points) | Unit 3 |

Questions and texts of the papers are never copied: every text and item here is written for this course in the formats above.

## Aim

The course practices each part of the paper in its own format, in the words of B1 and below, for students who have finished English B1 or are near it. It teaches nothing new: its own topics are practice-only, and the grammar, reading and writing topics it reuses keep their place in the graph.

## How to Read This Outline

Each unit lists the topics it reuses (*existing*) and its new topics. A new topic judged by rules has two knowledge points of rising difficulty with at least 30 items each; one judged by a model has 12 tasks with model answers. Each item of seven-from-five, cloze and grammar filling is one gap in a sentence or a short paragraph rather than a whole text with many gaps, so that every item has its own explanation; the paragraphs of seven-from-five come from ten short texts of seven paragraphs each, and the wrong options of a gap are other sentences of its text. Passages use only words of B1 and below, apart from names and terms they explain, and run to about 250 to 350 words, like the texts of the paper.

## Units

### 1. Reading

**Existing:** `main-idea`, `supporting-details`, `making-inferences`, `authors-purpose`, `identifying-topic-sentences`, `summarizing-a-text`, `ordering-sentences-in-a-paragraph`, `choosing-transitions`; the words of the themes the paper draws on, school, people, health, the environment, culture and science: `b1-words-school-life`, `b1-words-learning`, `b1-words-study-skills`, `b1-words-relationships`, `b1-words-emotions`, `b1-words-healthy-habits`, `b1-words-environment`, `b1-words-green-living`, `b1-words-culture`, `b1-words-science`, `b1-words-inventions`, `b1-words-describing-events`.

**New**

- `gaokao-seven-from-five` Seven-from-Five (practice-only; after `ordering-sentences-in-a-paragraph`, `choosing-transitions`). Choose the sentence that fits a gap in a short text: (1) a gap that opens or closes a paragraph; (2) a gap in the middle, decided by the sentences on both sides.

**Reading:** `gaokao-reading`, 8 passages of 250 to 350 words with four questions each, 32 in all, on the main idea, details, inferences and the writer's purpose: a notice of summer courses; a school's robot club; a grandmother's garden; how ants find food; a museum without walls; the boy who fixed bicycles; sleep and teenagers; a letter to a future self.

### 2. Language Use

**Existing:** `articles-in-general-and-specific`, `active-and-passive-voice`, `present-perfect-or-past-simple`, `present-perfect-continuous`, `past-perfect`, `verb-tense-consistency`, `first-conditional`, `second-conditional`, `may-might-and-could`, `gerunds-and-infinitives`, `relative-clauses`, `combining-with-relative-clauses`, `phrasal-verbs`, `comparatives-and-superlatives`; grammar filling comes before the cloze.

**New**

- `gaokao-cloze` Cloze (practice-only; after `phrasal-verbs`). Choose the word of four that fits a gap in a story: (1) words decided by the sentence; (2) words decided by the story around it.
- `gaokao-grammar-filling` Grammar Filling (practice-only; after `relative-clauses`, `active-and-passive-voice`, `past-perfect`). Type one word in a gap: (1) the right form of a given word: tense, passive, non-finite form, comparison, noun, adjective or adverb; (2) a gap with no word given: an article, a preposition, a pronoun, a conjunction or a relative word.

### 3. Writing

**Existing:** `writing-a-formal-email`, `writing-an-informal-email`, `writing-a-letter-of-advice`, `writing-a-letter-of-complaint`, `writing-an-article`, `b1-words-life-events`, `writing-a-story`.

**New**

- `gaokao-practical-writing` Practical Writing (judged by a model; practice-only; after `writing-a-formal-email`, `writing-an-informal-email`). An email, a letter, a notice, an invitation or a short report for the school newspaper, of about 80 words, that covers every point of the task.
- `gaokao-continuation-skills` Continuing a Story (practice-only; after `b1-words-emotions`, `ordering-sentences-in-a-paragraph`). The two skills a continuation rests on, judged by rules: (1) the sentence that follows a paragraph's given opening and agrees with the story, over 15 stories with two openings each; (2) the sentence that shows a feeling through action instead of naming it.
- `gaokao-continuation-writing` Continuation Writing (judged by a model; practice-only; after `writing-a-story`). Read a story and continue it in two paragraphs of about 150 words in all, each beginning with the given sentence, keeping its characters and leading to an ending.

**Later, with audio and recording:** the listening part, with conversations and talks on school life.
