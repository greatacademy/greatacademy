# Reading for Meaning

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §3 and §5. [简体中文](english-reading-comprehension.zh-CN.md).

- **Level:** Grades 3 to 5
- **Prerequisite(s):** Speaking English and reading simple paragraphs
- **Language of explanation:** English
- **Size:** 2 units · 5 modules · 15 topics

## Description

How to find what a text says, how texts are organized, and how to read between the lines. For learners who already speak English, in English.

## Units

### 1. Informational Texts (7 topics)

#### 1.1. Main Ideas

1.1.1. Main Idea  
1.1.2. Supporting Details and Summaries  
1.1.3. Text Features

#### 1.2. How Texts Are Organized

1.2.1. Sequence  
1.2.2. Cause and Effect  
1.2.3. Compare and Contrast  
1.2.4. Text Structures

### 2. Thinking About Texts (8 topics)

#### 2.1. Reading Between the Lines

2.1.1. Making Inferences  
2.1.2. Fact and Opinion  
2.1.3. Author's Purpose

#### 2.2. Stories

2.2.1. Point of View  
2.2.2. Characters  
2.2.3. The Lesson of a Story

#### 2.3. Figurative Language

2.3.1. Similes and Metaphors  
2.3.2. Idioms

## Reading

Two knowledge units of original passages, offered as each one's topics are learned:

1. How Animals Survive (10 informational passages: main ideas, organization, inference, fact and opinion, purpose)
2. Stories and Fables (10 stories: characters, point of view, lessons, figurative language)
