# Grammar and Sentence Writing

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §11. [简体中文](english-grammar-writing.zh-CN.md).

- **Level:** Grades 3 to 8
- **Prerequisite(s):** Speaking English and reading simple sentences
- **Language of explanation:** English
- **Size:** 3 units · 10 modules · 41 topics

## Description

How English sentences work, how to join and improve them, and how words are built from their parts. For learners who already speak English, in English.
