# Rhetoric and Composition

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §3 and §6.1. [简体中文](english-rhetoric-composition.zh-CN.md).

- **Level:** Grades 9 to 12
- **Prerequisite(s):** Literature and Argument
- **Language of explanation:** English
- **Size:** 3 units · 4 modules · 10 topics

## Description

How writers and speakers persuade, where reasoning fails, how literature works on a reader, and how to write about all of it from sources. For learners who already speak English, in English.

### 1. Rhetoric (5 topics)

#### 1.1. Persuasion

1.1.3. Rhetorical Devices

#### 1.2. Reasoning and Sources

1.2.1. Logical Fallacies

### 2. Literature (3 topics)

#### 2.1. Craft

2.1.1. Characterization  
2.1.2. Allusion

### 3. Composition (2 topics)

#### 3.1. Writing from Texts

3.1.1. Writing a Rhetorical Analysis  
3.1.2. Synthesizing Sources
