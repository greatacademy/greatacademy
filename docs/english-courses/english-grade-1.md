# English, Grade 1

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §6.3 and the [development plan](../english-development-plan.md) §E8. [简体中文](english-grade-1.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `english-grade-1`
- **Level:** Grade 1, for children whose first language is English, learning with an adult nearby
- **Prerequisite(s):** Knowing the letters and some of their sounds
- **Language of explanation:** English
- **Size:** 6 units · 48 topics, all new (17 phonics, 11 grammar and mechanics, 5 reading, 3 writing checked by rules, 6 listening, 6 speaking) · about 492 XP · 6 reading units of 18 passages

## Aim

By the end of grade 1 a child matches capital and small letters, puts letters and words in ABC order, and reads and spells words with short vowels, digraphs, blends and the common sight words; they hear these sounds in spoken words and read them aloud. They start a sentence with a capital, end it with a period or a question mark, capitalize names, days and places, and put words in order; they know naming, action and describing words, add *s* or *es* for more than one, choose *a* or *an*, put *he*, *she*, *it* and *they* in place of a noun, and join two ideas with *and*. In a short story they find who is in it, where it happens and what happens first, next and last; in a short text of facts they find the answer to a question; they hear rhymes and clap syllables. They write a whole sentence, add a describing word to it, and write steps in order with *first*, *next* and *last*.

## How to Read This Outline

Every unit is a theme and teaches phonics, grammar and mechanics, reading and, in some units, writing or words; it ends with a reading unit of three short passages on the theme, a listening topic on those passages and a topic of reading their sentences aloud. Each topic lists its prerequisites, which may lie in an earlier unit or in the previous grade, and its knowledge points as steps. There is no conversation and no writing judged by a model: a child of this age speaks by reading aloud, and every task is checked by rules.

Strands: of the 48 topics, 17 phonics (35%), 11 grammar and mechanics (23%), 5 reading (10%), 3 writing checked by rules (6%), 6 listening (12%), 6 speaking (12%).

Depth: each knowledge point of phonics, grammar, words, reading and writing has 30 hand-written items; topics of hearing and reading aloud take their items from the words and sentences of the phonics topics and the passages of the unit, recorded ahead of time. A phonics, grammar, reading or writing topic earns about 10 XP, a hearing or reading-aloud topic about 8 and a listening topic 15, so the lessons of the grade come to about 492 XP.

Listening and reading aloud use recordings made ahead of time, so they need no live voice service; the passages are short and decodable, with words the units have taught.

## What the Next Grade Builds On

Grade 2 builds on this year: phonics moves on to words with a silent e, vowel teams and whole short sentences; grammar to the past tense, *is*, *are*, *was* and *were*, owning with *'s*, comparing, plurals that change and contractions; reading to the beginning, middle and end of a story, the main topic of a text and how characters feel; and writing from single sentences to a story in order, a short report and a thank-you note.
