# Literature and Argument

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §3 and §6.1. [简体中文](english-literature-argument.zh-CN.md).

- **Level:** Grades 6 to 8
- **Prerequisite(s):** Reading for Meaning, Grammar and Sentence Writing
- **Language of explanation:** English
- **Size:** 2 units · 4 modules · 15 topics

## Description

How stories and poems make meaning, and how arguments are built from claims, evidence and reasoning. For learners who already speak English, in English.

## Units

### 1. Literature (5 topics)

#### 1.1. Language of Literature

1.1.1. Personification and Hyperbole  
1.1.2. Sound Devices

#### 1.2. Story and Meaning

1.2.1. Plot and Conflict  
1.2.2. Theme  
1.2.3. Tone and Mood

### 2. Argument (10 topics)

#### 2.1. Building an Argument

2.1.1. Claims  
2.1.2. Evidence  
2.1.3. Reasoning  
2.1.4. Counterclaims and Rebuttals

#### 2.2. Writing an Argument Essay

2.2.1. Writing a Thesis Statement  
2.2.2. Outlining an Argument  
2.2.3. Writing a Body Paragraph with Evidence  
2.2.4. Writing a Counterclaim Paragraph  
2.2.5. Introductions and Conclusions  
2.2.6. Revising a Draft

The topics of 2.2 are judged by a model, one move of an essay at a time; a learner whom no route may serve goes through the course without them.

## Reading

Two knowledge units of original passages, offered as each one's topics are learned:

1. Tales and Poems (10 stories, poems and letters: figurative language, sound, plot, theme, tone and mood)
2. Making the Case (10 arguments: claims, evidence, reasoning, counterclaims and rebuttals)
