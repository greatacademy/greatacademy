# English, Grade 2

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §6.3 and the [development plan](../english-development-plan.md) §E8. [简体中文](english-grade-2.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `english-grade-2`
- **Level:** Grade 2, for children whose first language is English or who read English as well as a native speaker of their age
- **Prerequisite(s):** Grade 1: reading words with short vowels, digraphs and blends, and writing a whole sentence
- **Language of explanation:** English
- **Size:** 6 units · 40 topics, all new (11 phonics, 2 vocabulary, 9 grammar and mechanics, 3 reading, 3 writing checked by rules, 6 listening, 6 speaking) · about 424 XP · 6 reading units of 18 passages

## Aim

By the end of grade 2 a child reads and spells words with a silent e and with vowel teams, reads short sentences aloud and writes them. They put verbs in the past with *-ed* and know the past verbs that change, choose *is*, *are*, *was* or *were*, show owning with *'s*, compare with *-er* and *-est*, know the plurals that change, write contractions and say what they stand for, join sentences with *and*, *but* and *or* with a comma before the joining word, and put commas in dates and in the greeting and closing of a letter. They use *un-* and *re-* to read new words and choose between stronger and weaker words. In a story or fable they find the beginning, the middle and the end, how the problem is solved and how a character feels and why; in a text of facts they find what it is mostly about and the best title. They group facts that belong together, tell a story in order with time words and a closing sentence, and write a thank-you note that says why.

## How to Read This Outline

Every unit is a theme and teaches phonics, grammar and mechanics, reading and, in some units, writing or words; it ends with a reading unit of three short passages on the theme, a listening topic on those passages and a topic of reading their sentences aloud. Each topic lists its prerequisites, which may lie in an earlier unit or in the previous grade, and its knowledge points as steps. There is no conversation and no writing judged by a model: a child of this age speaks by reading aloud, and every task is checked by rules.

Strands: of the 40 topics, 11 phonics (28%), 2 vocabulary (5%), 9 grammar and mechanics (22%), 3 reading (8%), 3 writing checked by rules (8%), 6 listening (15%), 6 speaking (15%).

Depth: each knowledge point of phonics, grammar, words, reading and writing has 30 hand-written items; topics of hearing and reading aloud take their items from the words and sentences of the phonics topics and the passages of the unit, recorded ahead of time. A phonics, grammar, reading or writing topic earns about 10 XP, a hearing or reading-aloud topic about 8 and a listening topic 15, so the lessons of the grade come to about 424 XP.

Listening and reading aloud use recordings made ahead of time, so they need no live voice service; the passages are short and decodable, with words the units have taught.

## What the Next Grade Builds On

Grade 3 builds on this year: words grow into themed batches with base words, prefixes, suffixes and compounds; grammar moves to nouns and pronouns, subjects and predicates, the simple tenses with agreement and conjunctions that join ideas; reading turns to inferences, characters who change, the lesson of a fable, the main idea and its supporting details; and writing grows from a few sentences into a paragraph with a topic sentence, details in order and a closing sentence.
