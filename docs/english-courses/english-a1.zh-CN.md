# 英语 初级（A1）

> 这是 Great Academy 自己的课程大纲。设计见[英语课程设计](../english-curriculum.zh-CN.md) §6.2。[English](english-a1.md)。

- **课程：** `english-a1`
- **级别：** CEFR A1，面向以英语为外语的学习者
- **先修：** 入门级，或掌握其中的词以及用 *be*、*have*、*can* 造的句子
- **讲解语言：** 界面语言：中文或英语
- **规模：** 12 个单元 · 71 个知识点（词汇 41 个，语法 19 个，写作 11 个）· 471 个词条，本级结束时累计 635 个

## 目标

学完初级，学习者能理解和使用熟悉的日常表达和非常简单的句子，能介绍自己和他人，能就住在哪里、认识的人和拥有的东西提问和回答（CEFR A1）。他们掌握约 600 个词，能读日常主题的短文，会写句子和问句；从图书馆单元起，还会写短便条、明信片和描述。

语法以现在时为主：一般现在时及其第三人称 *-s*、否定和疑问，以及现在进行时；然后是可数与不可数名词、*there is*、方位和时间介词，以及 *was* 和 *were*。这些正是说中文的学习者最容易出错的地方：中文动词不随人称和时间变化，问句保持陈述句的语序，名词也不区分单复数。

## 阅读说明

每个单元按技能分列知识点。词汇知识点教一批词条；括号中是本级及更高级别的文章也可以使用的词形。每个词在所有级别中只教一次，一篇文章只用本级及以下教过的词。语法和写作知识点列出先修；标为*已有*的知识点已在图中，保留自己的先修。写作知识点由规则或模型评判（§8）。阅读单元和对话在后续步骤中依照本大纲编写；听力和口语要等音频和录音服务就绪，每个单元说明届时增加的内容。

## 单元

### 1. 关于我

**词汇**

- `a1-words-numbers-to-hundred` 数字 13 到 100 (12): thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, hundred (hundreds)
- `a1-words-about-me` 我住在哪里 (11): country (countries), city (cities), live (lives, lived, living), from, speak (speaks, spoke, spoken, speaking), English, Chinese, language (languages), phone (phones), number (numbers), email (emails)
- `a1-words-feelings` 我的感受 (11): feel (feels, felt, feeling), fine, well, okay, great (greater, greatest), tired, sick, angry, afraid, now, again
- `a1-words-everyday-words` 日常用词 (12): thing (things), place (places), person, life, idea (ideas), part (parts), cry (cries, cried, crying), high (higher, highest), low (lower, lowest), sure, ready, real

**语法**

- `present-simple` 一般现在时（先修：`the-verb-be`、`a1-words-about-me`）. *I live in Beijing. We speak Chinese.* 一般现在时说明真实或经常的情况；主语是 *I*、*you*、*we*、*they* 时，动词不加词尾。

**阅读：** `a1-reading-all-about-me`，7 篇：从加拿大来的新同学；笔友；我们班说的语言；你多大了？；一个电话号码；我的城市；你今天感觉怎么样？。

**对话：** `introducing-yourself`。导师是一个在线课堂的老师，学生来自许多国家。 任务：说出你的名字、年龄、来自哪里以及会说哪些语言。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听别人自我介绍，记下他们的年龄和国家；说一百以内的数字并介绍自己。

### 2. 我的一天

**词汇**

- `a1-words-my-day` 我的一天 (12): get (gets, got, gotten, getting), up, wake (wakes, woke, woken, waking), go (goes, went, gone, going), come (comes, came, coming), home (homes), bed (beds), sleep (sleeps, slept, sleeping), breakfast (breakfasts), lunch (lunches), dinner (dinners), brush (brushes, brushed, brushing)
- `a1-words-times-of-day` 一天中的时段 (10): morning (mornings), afternoon (afternoons), evening (evenings), night (nights), day (days), today, every, then, after, before
- `a1-words-how-often` 多久一次 (11): always, usually, often, sometimes, never, start (starts, started, starting), finish (finishes, finished, finishing), work (works, worked, working), watch (watches, watched, watching), play (plays, played, playing), homework
- `a1-words-little-words-1` 小词 1 (10): for, out, down, off, over, by, into, just, really, why

**语法**

- `third-person-s` He plays, she watches（先修：`present-simple`、`he-she-and-it`）. 主语是 *he*、*she*、*it* 或单个的人时，一般现在时的动词要加 *-s* 或 *-es*：*she gets up*、*he watches*、*it goes*。中文动词从不变化，所以漏掉这个 *-s* 是 A1 最常见的错误。
- `adverbs-of-frequency` always、usually、never（先修：`present-simple`、`a1-words-how-often`）. *I always get up at seven.* 这些词放在动词前，但放在 *am*、*is*、*are* 之后：*She is never late.*

**写作**

- `writing-sentences-about-routines` 写日常作息（规则评判；先修：`third-person-s`、`writing-capitals-and-periods`）. 把笔记写成描述一天的句子：*Lily / get up / 7:00* 写成 *Lily gets up at seven o'clock.*

**阅读：** `a1-reading-my-day`，7 篇：忙碌的星期一；农夫的早晨；夜班护士；和爷爷一起吃早餐；放学后做作业；星期天不一样；我的狗的一天。

**对话：** `talking-about-your-day`。导师扮演一位在另一个国家的朋友。 任务：告诉朋友你几点起床、放学后做什么、几点睡觉，并问问对方的一天。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听别人描述一天并给图片排序；用 *always* 和 *never* 说出自己的作息。

### 3. 时间与日期

**词汇**

- `a1-words-time` 看时间 (12): time (times), clock (clocks), o'clock, half (halves), quarter (quarters), past, minute (minutes), hour (hours), week (weeks), weekend (weekends), late, early
- `a1-words-days` 星期 (10): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, month (months), next, last
- `a1-words-months` 月份 (12): January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December
- `a1-words-dates` 日期与生日 (12): sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety, zero, first, second (seconds), third, fourth, fifth, birthday (birthdays), date (dates)

**语法**

- `prepositions-of-time` 表示时间的 at、on 和 in（先修：`a1-words-days`、`a1-words-time`）. *at* seven o'clock、*on* Monday、*in* May：中文在这里不用词，或者三种情况都用同一个词。

**写作**

- `writing-capital-letters-for-names` 专有名词的大写（规则评判；先修：`writing-capitals-and-periods`、`a1-words-months`）. 人名、地名、语言、星期和月份以大写字母开头：*On Monday, Anna speaks English.*

**阅读：** `a1-reading-time-and-dates`，7 篇：Tom 的一周；一年中的生日；钟表和日历；八点半的公交车；一个月的周末；春天的第一天；又迟到了！。

**对话：** `making-a-plan-to-meet`。导师扮演一位这周想见面的朋友。 任务：商定见面的日期和时间，并说明其他时间为什么不行。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 在简短的留言中听时间和日期；说出时间和自己的生日。

### 4. 食物与饮料

**词汇**

- `a1-words-food` 食物 (12): food (foods), fruit (fruits), vegetable (vegetables), meat (meats), chicken (chickens), rice, bread (breads), egg (eggs), apple (apples), banana (bananas), potato (potatoes), tomato (tomatoes)
- `a1-words-drinks` 饮料 (12): water, milk, juice, tea, coffee, cup (cups), glass (glasses), bottle (bottles), drink (drinks, drank, drunk, drinking), hungry (hungrier, hungriest), thirsty, cake (cakes)
- `a1-words-meals` 餐食 (12): some, any, much, lot (lots), bowl (bowls), plate (plates), spoon (spoons), fork (forks), knife (knives), table (tables), cook (cooks, cooked, cooking), kitchen (kitchens)
- `a1-words-favorite-foods` 喜欢的食物 (12): sandwich (sandwiches), pizza (pizzas), noodle (noodles), dumpling (dumplings), soup (soups), salad (salads), ice, cream, candy (candies), cookie (cookies), sugar, salt

**语法**

- `countable-and-uncountable-nouns` 可数名词与不可数名词（先修：`plural-nouns-with-s`、`a1-words-food`）. *an apple, two apples*，但说 *some rice*、*some water*：有些名词不能数，不加 *-s*，也不用 *a*。
- `some-and-any` some 和 any（先修：`countable-and-uncountable-nouns`、`a1-words-meals`）. *There is some milk. Is there any juice? There isn't any bread.* 肯定句用 *some*，疑问句和否定句用 *any*。

**写作**

- `joining-with-and-but-or` 用 and、but、or 连接（规则评判；先修：`writing-words-in-order`）. 把两个短句连起来：*and* 表示补充，*but* 表示转折，*or* 表示选择。

**阅读：** `a1-reading-food`，7 篇：世界各地的早餐；做水果沙拉；厨房里有什么？；一个生日蛋糕；面条还是米饭？；学校的午饭；一只饥饿的兔子。

**对话：** `ordering-lunch`。导师扮演一家小咖啡馆的服务员。 任务：点一样吃的和一样喝的，再要一样你需要的东西。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听咖啡馆里的点餐并选出正确的餐盘；说食物名称和 *I'd like...*。

### 5. 衣服与购物

**词汇**

- `a1-words-clothes` 衣服 (12): clothes, shirt (shirts), T-shirt (t-shirts), dress (dresses), skirt (skirts), pants, shoe (shoes), sock (socks), hat (hats), coat (coats), jacket (jackets), wear (wears, wore, worn, wearing)
- `a1-words-shopping` 购物 (12): shop (shops, shopped, shopping), buy (buys, bought, buying), sell (sells, sold, selling), money, dollar (dollars), cheap (cheaper, cheapest), expensive, price (prices), store (stores), pay (pays, paid, paying), size (sizes), try (tries, tried, trying)
- `a1-words-at-the-shop` 哪一个？ (11): want (wants, wanted, wanting), would, new (newer, newest), another, other (others), same, different, only, all, more, most

**语法**

- `present-continuous` 现在进行时（先修：`the-verb-be`、`a1-words-clothes`）. *She is wearing a red coat.* 正在发生的事用 *am*、*is* 或 *are* 加动词的 *-ing* 形式；中文用“正在”，动词不变。
- `how-much-and-how-many` how much 和 how many（先修：`countable-and-uncountable-nouns`、`a1-words-shopping`）. *How many apples? How much milk? How much is it?* 可数的东西用 *many*，不可数的用 *much*，问价钱也用 *much*。

**写作**

- `writing-what-people-are-wearing` 写人们穿着什么（规则评判；先修：`present-continuous`、`writing-capitals-and-periods`）. 把笔记写成现在进行时的句子：*Mia / a blue dress* 写成 *Mia is wearing a blue dress.*

**阅读：** `a1-reading-shopping`，7 篇：在市场；过冬的新外套；校服；两美元还是十美元？；哪双鞋？；给妈妈的礼物；我们街上的商店。

**对话：** `buying-clothes`。导师在一家服装店工作。 任务：要一件你的尺码、你喜欢的颜色的衣服，问价钱，并决定买不买。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听价格并选出正确的金额；描述某人穿着什么。

### 6. 家

**词汇**

- `a1-words-home` 房间 (12): house (houses), room (rooms), bedroom (bedrooms), bathroom (bathrooms), door (doors), window (windows), wall (walls), floor (floors), garden (gardens), apartment (apartments), stairs, roof (roofs)
- `a1-words-things-at-home` 家里的东西 (12): sofa (sofas), lamp (lamps), picture (pictures), TV, computer (computers), toy (toys), cupboard (cupboards), between, behind, near, front, large (larger, largest)
- `a1-words-housework` 家务 (10): clean (cleans, cleaned, cleaning, cleaner, cleanest), wash (washes, washed, washing), help (helps, helped, helping), dish (dishes), make (makes, made, making), put (puts, putting), use (uses, used, using), dirty (dirtier, dirtiest), own (owns, owned, owning), towel (towels)

**语法**

- `there-is-and-there-are` there is 和 there are（先修：`plural-nouns-with-s`、`a1-words-home`）. *There is a lamp. There are two beds.* 英语用 *there is* 表示存在，中文说“有”；学习者常写成 *Have a lamp in my room.*
- `prepositions-of-place` next to、behind 和 between（先修：`in-on-and-under`、`a1-words-things-at-home`）. *The lamp is next to the bed. The cat is between the chairs.*

**写作**

- `writing-about-a-room` 描写一个房间（规则评判；先修：`there-is-and-there-are`、`prepositions-of-place`）. 把笔记写成句子：*a desk / next to the window* 写成 *There is a desk next to the window.*

**阅读：** `a1-reading-home`，7 篇：我们的新公寓；我的卧室；海边的房子；谁打扫厨房？；猫在哪里？；带大花园的小房子；搬家那天。

**对话：** `describing-your-home`。导师扮演一位下周要来拜访你的朋友。 任务：描述你家的房间，并说出你的卧室在哪里。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听描述并选出正确的房间；说出东西在哪里。

### 7. 业余时间

**词汇**

- `a1-words-hobbies` 爱好 (12): hobby (hobbies), guitar (guitars), piano (pianos), paint (paints, painted, painting), photo (photos), camera (cameras), fun, together, favorite, team (teams), park (parks), free

**写作**

### 8. 学校

**词汇**

- `a1-words-school-subjects` 学科 (12): math, science (sciences), art (arts), history, class (classes), lesson (lessons), subject (subjects), test (tests), learn (learns, learned, learning), teach (teaches, taught, teaching), easy (easier, easiest), difficult
- `a1-words-schoolwork` 功课 (12): student (students), question (questions), answer (answers, answered, answering), ask (asks, asked, asking), word (words), page (pages), paper (papers), spell (spells, spelled, spelling), understand (understands, understood, understanding), know (knows, knew, known, knowing), right, wrong
- `a1-words-little-words-2` 小词 2 (11): which, because, so, if, than, something, someone, anything, nothing, everyone, everything

**语法**

- `present-simple-negatives` don't 和 doesn't（先修：`third-person-s`）. *I don't like math. She doesn't like art.* 在 *doesn't* 后面，动词去掉 *-s*：不说 *She doesn't likes*。
- `present-simple-questions` 用 do 和 does 提问（先修：`third-person-s`）. *Do you like science? Does he speak English? Yes, he does.* 疑问句以 *do* 或 *does* 开头；中文保持语序，只在句末加“吗”。

**写作**

- `writing-questions` 写问句（规则评判；先修：`present-simple-questions`、`questions-with-be`）. 写出与答句对应的问句：*Yes, she does. She likes art.* 对应 *Does she like art?*

**阅读：** `a1-reading-school`，7 篇：山里的学校；我最喜欢的科目；科学测验；新老师；课堂上的问题；星期五的美术课；大家都来帮忙。

**对话：** `asking-about-school`。导师扮演从另一所学校转来的新同学。 任务：问对方喜欢和不喜欢哪些科目，并介绍你的学校。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听课程表并回答问题；大声问答 *Do you...?*。

### 9. 图书馆

**词汇**

- `a1-words-library` 图书馆 (12): library (libraries), borrow (borrows, borrowed, borrowing), return (returns, returned, returning), shelf (shelves), card (cards), due, story (stories), librarian (librarians), quiet (quieter, quietest), find (finds, found, finding), need (needs, needed, needing), back
- `a1-words-books` 书 (10): comic (comics), dictionary (dictionaries), title (titles), about, project (projects), bring (brings, brought, bringing), take (takes, took, taken, taking), give (gives, gave, given, giving), must, when

**语法**

- `object-pronouns` me、him、her 和 them（先修：`my-your-his-her`、`a1-words-books`）. *Can you help me? I like her. Give them to us.* 在动词或 *to*、*with* 这样的词后面，*I* 变成 *me*，*he* 变成 *him*，*they* 变成 *them*。
- `the-article-the` a 和 the（先修：`a-and-an`、`a1-words-library`）. *I have a book. The book is about cats.* *a* 指许多中的一个，*the* 指双方都知道的那一个。

**写作**

- `writing-a-short-note` 写便条（模型评判；先修：`joining-with-and-but-or`、`writing-capital-letters-for-names`、`object-pronouns`）. 根据任务写一张两到四句话的便条：说你在哪里、请人帮忙或者向人道谢。

**阅读：** `a1-reading-library`，7 篇：在镇图书馆的一天；丢了借书证；故事角；书星期五到期；安静的阅览室；给 Sam 的词典；图书管理员最喜欢的书。

**对话：** `library-help-desk`。导师扮演学校图书馆服务台的图书管理员（已有的对话）。 任务：说出你的课题是关于什么的，请对方帮你找一本相关的书，并问清楚什么时候还书。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听图书管理员说位置和日期；问 *Can I borrow...?* 和 *When is it due?*。

### 10. 天气与自然

**词汇**

- `a1-words-weather` 天气 (12): weather, sun (suns), sunny (sunnier, sunniest), rain (rains, rained, raining), rainy, snow (snows, snowed, snowing), snowy, wind (winds), windy, cloud (clouds), cloudy, cold (colder, coldest)
- `a1-words-seasons` 季节 (11): hot (hotter, hottest), warm (warmer, warmest), cool (cooler, coolest), season (seasons), spring (springs), summer (summers), fall (falls, fell, fallen, falling), winter (winters), sky (skies), umbrella (umbrellas), outside
- `a1-words-nature` 自然 (12): tree (trees), flower (flowers), grass, river (rivers), mountain (mountains), sea (seas), lake (lakes), hill (hills), leaf (leaves), beach (beaches), island (islands), forest (forests)
- `a1-words-wild-animals` 野生动物 (12): lion (lions), tiger (tigers), elephant (elephants), monkey (monkeys), bear (bears), snake (snakes), sheep, zoo (zoos), panda (pandas), giraffe (giraffes), wild, insect (insects)

**语法**

- `it-for-weather` It is raining（先修：`present-continuous`、`a1-words-weather`）. *It is cold. It's raining.* 英语谈天气和时间时需要 *it*，中文则直接用动词（“下雨了”）。

**写作**

- `writing-a-postcard` 写明信片（模型评判；先修：`it-for-weather`、`writing-a-short-note`）. 写一张三到五句话的明信片：你在哪里、天气怎么样、你正在做什么。

**阅读：** `a1-reading-weather`，7 篇：一座城市的四季；下雨天在家；下雪了！；海滩上的一天；动物怎样过冬；有风的小山；在森林里散步。

**对话：** `talking-about-the-weather`。导师扮演在另一座城市和你视频通话的朋友。 任务：说说你那里的天气和你正在做什么，再问问对方那里的天气。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听天气预报并选出图片；说出天气情况。

### 11. 人与职业

**词汇**

- `a1-words-work-actions` 工作中的动作 (10): build (builds, built, building), drive (drives, drove, driven, driving), fix (fixes, fixed, fixing), grow (grows, grew, grown, growing), carry (carries, carried, carrying), wait (waits, waited, waiting), call (calls, called, calling), meet (meets, met, meeting), keep (keeps, kept, keeping), show (shows, showed, shown, showing)

**语法**

- `was-and-were` was 和 were（先修：`the-verb-be`、`a1-words-jobs`）. *My grandpa was a farmer. They were busy yesterday.* *am* 和 *is* 的过去式是 *was*，*are* 的过去式是 *were*。

**写作**

- `writing-about-a-person` 描写一个人（模型评判；先修：`third-person-s`、`have-and-has`、`a1-words-describing-people`、`writing-a-short-note`）. 写四到六句话介绍一个你认识的人：他是谁、长什么样、做什么、喜欢什么。

**阅读：** `a1-reading-jobs`，7 篇：护士的一天；爷爷以前是农民；我们镇上的职业；公交车司机；医生忙碌的早晨；谁盖房子？；我的阿姨在工厂工作。

**对话：** `asking-about-a-job`。导师扮演职业日来班上的一位工作者。 任务：问清楚对方做什么工作、在哪里工作、每天做什么。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听别人描述自己的工作并与图片配对；描述一个人。

### 12. 城镇与出行

**词汇**

- `a1-words-town` 城镇 (12): town (towns), street (streets), road (roads), bus (buses), car (cars), train (trains), station (stations), bank (banks), restaurant (restaurants), hotel (hotels), museum (museums), map (maps)
- `a1-words-directions` 方向 (12): left, turn (turns, turned, turning), straight, stop (stops, stopped, stopping), cross (crosses, crossed, crossing), corner (corners), far (farther, farthest), way (ways), ticket (tickets), travel (travels, traveled, traveling), visit (visits, visited, visiting), plane (planes)
- `a1-words-trips` 旅行 (11): trip (trips), holiday (holidays), airport (airports), bridge (bridges), boat (boats), ship (ships), taxi (taxis), arrive (arrives, arrived, arriving), leave (leaving), fast (faster, fastest), slow (slower, slowest)

**语法**

- `wh-questions` where、when、what 引导的问句（先修：`present-simple-questions`、`questions-with-be`）. *Where do you live? When does the train leave?* 疑问词放在句首，后面接 *do* 或 *does*；中文的疑问词留在原位（“你住在哪儿？”）。
- `can-for-requests` Can I...? Can you...?（先修：`can-for-ability`、`a1-words-directions`）. *Can I have a ticket, please? Can you tell me the way?* *can* 也用来要东西和请人帮忙。

**写作**

- `writing-directions-to-your-home` 写路线（模型评判；先修：`imperatives`、`prepositions-of-place`、`a1-words-directions`、`writing-a-short-note`）. 写一条四到六句话的消息，告诉朋友怎样从任务给出的地方到你家。

**阅读：** `a1-reading-town`，7 篇：我们镇的地图；在城里迷路；坐火车去海边；博物馆在银行旁边；坐出租车去机场；在拐角处向左转；乘船游览。

**对话：** `asking-the-way`。导师扮演你所游览的小镇街上的一个人。 任务：问去车站的路，确认一遍路线，然后道谢。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听路线说明并在地图上找路；问路和指路。
