# TOEFL iBT

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §6.2. [简体中文](english-toefl.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `english-toefl`, in the group English Exams
- **Level:** CEFR B2 and above, for learners of English as a foreign language preparing for the TOEFL iBT
- **Prerequisite(s):** English B2 (`english-b2`)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 2 units · 13 topics (4 new, all practice-only) · 1 reading unit of 8 passages

## The Test

Checked on 2026-11-10 against the official descriptions of the updated test given since January 2026: [ets.org: Reading section](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content/reading.html), [ets.org: Writing section](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content/writing.html) and the [Updated TOEFL iBT Test Overview](https://www.eu.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-overview.pdf).

| Section | Format | In this course |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reading | Complete the Words: finish words whose second half is missing in a short academic text. Read in Daily Life: short everyday texts such as notices, messages and short informational texts, with questions on main ideas, details and implied meaning. Read an Academic Passage: a short academic passage (about 200 words), with questions on main ideas, details and vocabulary | Unit 1 |
| Writing | Build a Sentence: arrange words and phrases into a grammatical sentence or question that answers another. Write an Email: a request, information or a proposed solution in an academic or social situation (7 minutes). Write for an Academic Discussion: state and support an opinion in an online class discussion (10 minutes) | Unit 2 |
| Listening, Speaking | Conversations, announcements and talks; repeating sentences and a short interview | Not yet: see below |

Scores are reported in bands from 1 to 6, in steps of 0.5, for each section and in total; the total is the average of the four sections rounded to the nearest half band. Checked on 2026-11-12 against [ets.org: the score scale update](https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html), ETS aligns the bands with the CEFR the same way in every section:

| Band | 1 to 1.5 | 2 to 2.5 | 3 to 3.5 | 4 to 4.5 | 5 to 5.5 | 6 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| CEFR | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 | C1 | C2 |

Until January 2028 the score report also gives the CEFR level and a comparable total on the former 0 to 120 scale ([ets.org: score displays in 2026](https://www.ets.org/toefl/blog/understanding-toefl-ibt-score-displays.html)); ETS recommends a 4 where 80 was required, 4.5 for 90 and 5 for 100. Finishing English B2 aims at band 4; this course aims at 4.5 and above.

Questions, texts and word lists of the test are never copied: every text and item here is written for this course in the formats above.

## Aim

The course practices the short, quick tasks of the updated TOEFL: reading words from their first half and their context, reading everyday notices and short academic texts for what they say and imply, ordering a reply word by word, and writing an email and a post to a class discussion in a few minutes. It teaches nothing new. Its own topics are practice-only: a lesson starts with practice, and the explanation appears only after a failed attempt.

## How to Read This Outline

Each unit lists the topics it reuses (*existing*) and its new topics. A new topic judged by rules has two knowledge points of rising difficulty with at least 30 items each; one judged by a model has 12 tasks with model answers. Passages use only words of B2 and below, apart from names and terms they explain.

## Units

### 1. Reading

**Existing:** `main-idea`, `supporting-details`, `making-inferences`, `authors-purpose`, `reading-workplace-documents`.

**New**

- `toefl-complete-the-words` Complete the Words (practice-only; after `b2-words-linking-1`, `b2-words-research-2`). A short academic text with one word cut after its first half: (1) everyday and academic words of up to seven letters; (2) longer words, whose form depends on the sentence.

**Reading:** `toefl-reading`, 8 passages: four everyday texts of 100 to 160 words (a library notice, an email from a professor, a housing message, a poster for a club) and four academic passages of about 200 words (how bats find their way, the first maps, why bread rises, the coral and the alga).

**Later, with audio and recording:** Listen and Choose a Response, conversations, announcements and academic talks; Listen and Repeat and the Take an Interview task of Speaking.

### 2. Writing

**Existing:** `indirect-questions`, `subject-and-object-questions`, `writing-a-formal-email`, `writing-an-opinion-paragraph`, `counterclaims-and-rebuttals`.

**New**

- `toefl-build-a-sentence` Build a Sentence (practice-only; after `indirect-questions`, `subject-and-object-questions`). Order words and phrases into a reply to another speaker: (1) statements; (2) questions and indirect questions.
- `toefl-writing-an-email` Writing an Email (judged by a model; practice-only; after `writing-a-formal-email`). An email of about 100 words or more that does each thing the situation asks, to a professor, a classmate or an office.
- `toefl-academic-discussion` Writing for an Academic Discussion (judged by a model; practice-only; after `writing-an-opinion-paragraph`, `counterclaims-and-rebuttals`). A post of about 100 words or more that answers the professor's question with a clear opinion and a reason, and adds something to what two classmates said.
