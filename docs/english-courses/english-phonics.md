# Phonics, Kindergarten to Grade 2

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [English curriculum](../english-curriculum.md) §6 and the [development plan](../english-development-plan.md) §E6. [简体中文](english-phonics.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `english-phonics`
- **Level:** Kindergarten to grade 2, for children learning to read English, with an adult nearby
- **Prerequisite(s):** None
- **Language of explanation:** The interface's, so that a guardian can read the explanations in their own language
- **Size:** 3 units · 7 topics (all new: 5 of reading, 2 of writing checked by rules) · 64 XP · 69 pictures

## Aim

By the end of the course a child knows the capital and small form of every letter, says the alphabet in order and puts words in ABC order, names the first and last letters of a picture's word, reads three-letter words with a short vowel by sounding out and blending their letters, and spells them, then reads and spells words in which two letters, *sh*, *ch*, *th* or *ck*, make one sound. The course then grows to consonant blends, long vowels, sight words and short decodable sentences, and with audio to sounds heard and spoken: rhyme, blending and segmenting by ear, and reading aloud.

## How to Read This Outline

The course starts with the side of phonics that can be practiced from print and pictures, so it needs no audio. Each picture is a line drawing made for this course with no words in it, so a child must read the word to match it; only the numbers 6 and 10 are written. Explanations ask an adult to say words with the child where a sound must be heard. Every topic is new and lists its prerequisites.

Depth: each knowledge point has 30 to 117 distinct items. The 39 short-vowel picture words each have six near spellings that change one letter: two the vowel, two the first letter and two the last, so a wrong option always differs from the right word by one letter. The 30 digraph picture words each have three: one with another digraph, one with only one letter of its digraph, and one with another vowel.

## Units

### 1. Letters

- `capital-and-small-letters` Capital and Small Letters. Steps: choosing the small letter of a capital, among letters a beginner mixes it up with (*b*, *d*, *p*, *q*); then choosing a word written in capitals that is the same word.
- `alphabetical-order` ABC Order (after `capital-and-small-letters`). Steps: choosing the letter that comes after three in a row, in small letters and in capitals; then putting three picture words in ABC order by their first letters.
- `first-and-last-letters` First and Last Letters (after `capital-and-small-letters`). Steps: choosing the letter a picture's word begins with; then the letter missing at its end.

### 2. Short Vowels

- `reading-short-vowel-words` Reading Words with Short Vowels (after `first-and-last-letters`). Steps: choosing the word that names a picture, among words one letter away; then choosing the picture a word names, among pictures whose words share its letters.
- `spelling-short-vowel-words` Spelling Words with Short Vowels (writing; after `reading-short-vowel-words`). Steps: typing the vowel missing from the middle of a picture's word; then typing the whole word.

### 3. Digraphs

- `reading-words-with-digraphs` Reading Words with Digraphs (after `reading-short-vowel-words`). Steps: choosing the word that names a picture, among spellings with another digraph, one letter of the digraph, or another vowel; then choosing the picture a word names, among pictures whose words share its digraph.
- `spelling-words-with-digraphs` Spelling Words with Digraphs (writing; after `reading-words-with-digraphs` and `spelling-short-vowel-words`). Steps: typing the two letters missing from a picture's word; then typing the whole word.
