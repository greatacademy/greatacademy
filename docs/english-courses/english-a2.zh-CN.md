# 英语 基础级（A2）

> 这是 Great Academy 自己的课程大纲。设计见[英语课程设计](../english-curriculum.zh-CN.md) §6.2。[English](english-a2.md)。

- **课程：** `english-a2`
- **级别：** CEFR A2，面向以英语为外语的学习者
- **先修：** 初级，或掌握一般现在时、现在进行时、*was* 和 *were* 以及约 600 个词
- **讲解语言：** 界面语言：中文或英语
- **规模：** 12 个单元 · 88 个知识点（词汇 51 个，语法 22 个，写作 15 个）· 592 个词条，本级结束时累计 1245 个

## 目标

学完基础级，学习者能理解与切身相关领域（家庭、购物、周边环境、工作）的句子和常用表达，能用简单的语言描述自己的背景、周围环境和过去的事（CEFR A2）。他们掌握约 1,200 个词，能读 100 到 220 词的文章，会写简短的电子邮件、故事、描述和留言。

语法增加过去和将来：规则与不规则动词的一般过去时及其疑问和否定、过去进行时、*going to* 和 *will*、带 *ever*、*never*、*just*、*already*、*yet* 的现在完成时；然后是比较、副词、*should*、*must* 和 *have to*、所有格和数量。写作从句子走向由模型评判的短文。

## 阅读说明

每个单元按技能分列知识点。词汇知识点教一批词条；括号中是本级及更高级别的文章也可以使用的词形。每个词在所有级别中只教一次，一篇文章只用本级及以下教过的词。语法和写作知识点列出先修；标为*已有*的知识点已在图中，保留自己的先修。写作知识点由规则或模型评判（§8）。阅读单元和对话在后续步骤中依照本大纲编写；听力和口语要等音频和录音服务就绪，每个单元说明届时增加的内容。

## 单元

### 1. 上个周末

**词汇**

- `a2-words-past-time` 表示过去的词 (10): yesterday, ago, once, recently, soon, suddenly, finally, during, while, until
- `a2-words-relatives` 亲戚 (12): uncle (uncles), aunt (aunts), cousin (cousins), parent (parents), grandparent (grandparents), son (sons), daughter (daughters), husband (husbands), wife (wives), relative (relatives), married, born
- `a2-words-events` 发生的事 (12): happen (happens, happened, happening), event (events), plan (plans, planned, planning), decide (decides, decided, deciding), forget (forgets, forgot, forgotten, forgetting), remember (remembers, remembered, remembering), lose (loses, lost, losing), win (wins, won, winning), break (breaks, broke, broken, breaking), catch (catches, caught, catching), drop (drops, dropped, dropping), hurry (hurries, hurried, hurrying)
- `a2-words-weekend` 周末 (12): relax (relaxes, relaxed, relaxing), spend (spends, spent, spending), rest (rests, rested, resting), picnic (picnics), camp (camps, camped, camping), tent (tents), hike (hikes, hiked, hiking), gallery (galleries), crowd (crowds), bored, boring, interesting
- `a2-words-useful-words` 常用词 (12): important, reason (reasons), moment (moments), follow (follows, followed, following), possible, impossible, able, whole, side (sides), sentence (sentences), example (examples), difference (differences)

**语法**

- `past-simple-regular` 一般过去时：规则动词（先修：`was-and-were`、`present-simple`）. *We played. She visited.* 规则动词对所有人称都加 *-ed*；中文用“了”或时间词表示过去，动词本身不变。
- `past-simple-irregular` 一般过去时：不规则动词（先修：`past-simple-regular`）. *go, went; see, saw; buy, bought.* 最常用的动词有自己的过去式，要一个一个记。
- `past-simple-questions-and-negatives` did 和 didn't（先修：`past-simple-irregular`、`present-simple-questions`）. *Did you go? I didn't see it.* 在 *did* 和 *didn't* 后面，动词用原形：不说 *Did you went?*

**写作**

- `writing-sentences-in-the-past` 用过去时写句子（规则评判；先修：`past-simple-irregular`、`writing-sentences-about-routines`）. 把关于上周末的笔记写成一般过去时的句子：*Sam / go / the zoo / Saturday* 写成 *Sam went to the zoo on Saturday.*
- `writing-about-your-weekend` 写你的周末（模型评判；先修：`writing-sentences-in-the-past`、`writing-a-short-note`）. 用一般过去时写五到七句话，按发生的顺序讲你上周末做了什么。

**阅读：** `a2-reading-last-weekend`，8 篇：出了岔子的野餐；在湖边露营；去奶奶的村子；丢失的帐篷；下雨的星期六在美术馆；表兄妹在海滩；我忘带钥匙的那天；给爸爸的惊喜。

**对话：** `telling-about-your-weekend`。导师扮演星期一早上的同学。 任务：讲讲你上周末做了什么，问问对方做了什么，并对对方的事作出回应。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听一个周末的故事并给事件排序；用一般过去时讲述自己的周末，读准 *-ed* 的发音。

### 2. 健康与身体

**词汇**

- `a2-words-health` 生病 (12): health, healthy (healthier, healthiest), ill, pain (pains), headache (headaches), toothache (toothaches), stomach (stomachs), cough (coughs, coughed, coughing), fever (fevers), medicine (medicines), dentist (dentists), hurt (hurts, hurting)
- `a2-words-body-2` 更多身体部位 (11): neck (necks), shoulder (shoulders), knee (knees), finger (fingers), toe (toes), tooth (teeth), heart (hearts), skin, blood, bone (bones), throat (throats)
- `a2-words-staying-well` 保持健康 (11): exercise (exercises, exercised, exercising), diet (diets), fit (fitter, fittest), strong (stronger, strongest), weak (weaker, weakest), habit (habits), should, matter (matters, mattered, mattering), advice, careful, accident (accidents)
- `a2-words-at-the-doctor` 看医生 (12): appointment (appointments), problem (problems), temperature (temperatures), pill (pills), pharmacy (pharmacies), ambulance (ambulances), emergency (emergencies), patient (patients), check (checks, checked, checking), serious, terrible, worry (worries, worried, worrying)

**语法**

- `should-and-must` should 和 must（先修：`can-for-ability`、`a2-words-staying-well`）. *You should rest. You must take this medicine.* *should* 表示建议，*must* 表示必须；两者后面都不加 *to*。
- `have-to` have to 和 don't have to（先修：`should-and-must`、`third-person-s`）. *She has to stay in bed. You don't have to come.* *don't have to* 表示不必，而不是不允许。

**写作**

- `writing-advice-with-should` 写建议（规则评判；先修：`should-and-must`、`joining-with-and-but-or`）. 把问题和提示写成建议：*a headache / drink water* 写成 *You should drink some water.*

**阅读：** `a2-reading-health`，8 篇：看牙医；我们为什么需要睡眠；自行车事故；上学日的健康习惯；大赛前发烧；结实的骨头；救护车来的那个晚上；爷爷的新饮食。

**对话：** `at-the-doctor`。导师扮演诊所里的医生。 任务：说出哪里不舒服、从什么时候开始，回答医生的问题，并确认你该怎么做。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听医生的建议并选出病人必须做的事；描述病痛并寻求建议。

- `a2-words-pronouns` mine、yours 和 myself (12): mine, yours, hers, ours, theirs, myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves

**语法**

- `comparatives-and-superlatives` 比较级和最高级（先修：`adjectives-and-adverbs`，*已有*）. *bigger than, the biggest; more famous than, the most famous.* 中文加“比”或“最”，形容词本身不变。
- `possessive-pronouns` mine、yours 和 theirs（先修：`my-your-his-her`、`a2-words-pronouns`）. *This bag is mine. Is that yours?* *mine* 这样的词单独使用，后面不接名词。
- `apostrophes-and-possessives` 撇号和所有格（先修：`nouns-and-pronouns`，*已有*）. *Anna's bike, the neighbors' garden*：人名后的 *'s* 相当于“的”。

**写作**

- `writing-comparisons` 写比较句（规则评判；先修：`comparatives-and-superlatives`、`writing-capitals-and-periods`）. 把笔记写成比较句：*the park / the square / quiet* 写成 *The park is quieter than the square.*
- `writing-a-description-of-a-place` 描写一个地方（模型评判；先修：`writing-comparisons`、`writing-about-a-room`）. 用六到八句话描写你的街区或任务给出的地方：在哪里、有什么、是什么样子。

**阅读：** `a2-reading-neighborhood`，8 篇：广场上新开的面包店；分享的村庄；我们吵闹的街道；操场志愿者；李太太的花园；门口的陌生人；镇上最安静的地方；欢迎来到街区。

**对话：** `meeting-a-new-neighbor`。导师扮演刚搬来的邻居。 任务：欢迎对方，告诉对方有用的地方在哪里，并主动提供帮助。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听对一个城镇的描述并在地图上标出地点；口头比较两个地方。

**语法**

- `present-perfect-with-ever-and-never` Have you ever...?（先修：`past-simple-irregular`、`have-and-has`）. *Have you ever seen a waterfall? I have never climbed a mountain.* 现在完成时由 *have* 加过去分词构成，表示到现在为止任何时候的经历。

**写作**

**阅读：** `a2-reading-nature`，8 篇：天空为什么是蓝的；丛林里的生活；星空下的一夜；种子怎样旅行；瀑布小路；池塘结冰的时候；太空中的宇航员；暴风雨后的彩虹。

**对话：** `planning-a-day-outdoors`。导师扮演和你一起计划出游的朋友。 任务：商定去哪里，了解天气，并决定带什么。

**有了音频和录音之后：** 听自然节目并选出正确的句子；描述大自然中的一个地方。
