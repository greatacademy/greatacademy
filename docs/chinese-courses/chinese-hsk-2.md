# HSK 2

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md) §8.5. [简体中文](chinese-hsk-2.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-hsk-2`, in the group Chinese Exams
- **Level:** Level 2 of the new HSK, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 1](chinese-level-1.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 14 units · 224 topics (7 new, all practice-only) · 555 knowledge points

## The Test

The new HSK has nine levels built on the *Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for International Chinese Language Education* (GF 0025-2021), which level 2 of this subject follows. In the structure of the papers the test owner published in December 2025, HSK 2 has 60 items: listening of 25 in three parts, reading of 25 in four, and writing of 10 in two, the second writing characters from their pinyin. This course practises what each part asks in some form: the word that fills a sentence, the meaning of a word, the character of a word, characters written from their pinyin, building sentences, words and sentences heard, repeating and reading aloud. Questions, texts and word lists of the test are never copied: every item here comes from the data of level 2 or is written for this course.

## Aim

The course turns level 2 into test practice. It holds the units of [Chinese Level 2](chinese-level-2.md), so a learner who has not taken it learns it here, and then seven exam topics that mix the words and characters of every theme, as a test does, rather than one batch at a time. It teaches nothing new. Its own topics are practice-only: a lesson starts with practice, and the explanation appears only after a failed attempt. The topics it reuses keep their place in the graph, so a learner who has learned them in the level course is credited and moves on.

## Units

1 to 13. The units of [Chinese Level 2](chinese-level-2.md), unchanged, its unit of writing characters by hand last.

14. **Exam Practice**

- `hsk-2-gaps` HSK 2: Words in Sentences (practice-only; after the last word topic of level 2). Every sentence of level 2 with its gap: (1) five words to choose from, all of its batch, none of those kept apart from it; (2) six, or five where the batch has no more that could not also fit.
- `hsk-2-meanings` HSK 2: Words and Meanings (practice-only). Every word of level 2: (1) the word of a meaning among four; (2) the meaning of a word among four, in the learner's language.
- `hsk-2-characters` HSK 2: Characters (practice-only). Every character of level 2: (1) by its reading and meaning, among characters that look like it; (2) the character missing from a word of level 2, given the word's pinyin and meaning, among the same, leaving out a word whose meaning would give it away.
- `hsk-2-written-characters` HSK 2: Characters from Pinyin (practice-only; after the last handwriting topic of level 2). Each character level 2 writes by hand, written stroke by stroke where its pinyin stands: (1) in a word of the levels; (2) in a sentence of the levels, as the writing paper asks.
- `hsk-2-sentences` HSK 2: Building Sentences (practice-only; after the last sentence-building topic of level 2). The sentences of the sentence-building topics of level 2, mixed, each topic in turn: (1) short ones of three or four parts; (2) longer ones of five to seven.
- `hsk-2-listening` HSK 2: Listening (practice-only; after the last listening topic of level 2). The words and sentences of every unit of level 2, heard without being seen, the units taking turns: (1) the meaning of a word heard, among three; (2) the word heard in a sentence, among three.
- `hsk-2-speaking` HSK 2: Speaking (practice-only; after the last speaking topic of level 2). The same words and sentences, heard by a speech service: (1) a word said after its recording; (2) a sentence read aloud.

**Not yet practised:** the paper's other kinds of listening and reading, such as dialogues, talks and passages with questions, and the spoken tasks of the HSKK beyond repeating and reading aloud.
