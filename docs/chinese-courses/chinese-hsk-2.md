# HSK 2

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md) §8.5. [简体中文](chinese-hsk-2.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-hsk-2`, in the group Chinese Exams
- **Level:** Level 2 of the new HSK, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 1](chinese-level-1.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 13 units · 186 topics (3 new, all practice-only) · 479 knowledge points

## The Test

The new HSK has nine levels built on the *Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for International Chinese Language Education* (GF 0025-2021), which level 2 of this subject follow. The question types of each level's papers are still to be confirmed from the test owner's own syllabus and sample papers (C6, a person's step); until then this course practises what every level's reading paper asks in some form: the word that fills a sentence, the meaning of a word, and the character of a word. Questions, texts and word lists of the test are never copied: every item here comes from the data of level 2.

## Aim

The course turns level 2 into test practice. It holds the units of [Chinese Level 2](chinese-level-2.md), so a learner who has not taken it learns it here, and then three exam topics that mix the words and characters of every theme, as a test does, rather than one batch at a time. It teaches nothing new. Its own topics are practice-only: a lesson starts with practice, and the explanation appears only after a failed attempt. The topics it reuses keep their place in the graph, so a learner who has learned them in the level course is credited and moves on.

## Units

1 to 12. The units of [Chinese Level 2](chinese-level-2.md), unchanged.

13. **Exam Practice**

- `hsk-2-gaps` HSK 2: Words in Sentences (practice-only; after the last word topic of level 2). Every sentence of level 2 with its gap: (1) five words to choose from, all of its batch, none of those kept apart from it; (2) six, or five where the batch has no more that could not also fit.
- `hsk-2-meanings` HSK 2: Words and Meanings (practice-only). Every word of level 2: (1) the word of a meaning among four; (2) the meaning of a word among four, in the learner's language.
- `hsk-2-characters` HSK 2: Characters (practice-only). Every character of level 2: (1) by its reading and meaning, among characters that look like it; (2) the character missing from a word of level 2, given the word's pinyin and meaning, among the same, leaving out a word whose meaning would give it away.

**Later, with audio and judged answers:** listening, speaking and writing, once the papers' question types are confirmed.
