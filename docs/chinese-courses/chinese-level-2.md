# Chinese Level 2

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md). [简体中文](chinese-level-2.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-level-2`
- **Level:** Level 2, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 1](chinese-level-1.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 12 units · 107 topics (30 of characters, 77 of words) · 321 knowledge points

## Aim

Level 2 teaches 300 more characters and 770 words of daily life, in twelve themed units from the daily routine and the home to travel, feelings, nature and festivals. Characters and words are learned as in level 1, and every sentence is written only with characters already taught. The characters were chosen to be close to levels 2 and 3 of the standard (GF 0025-2021); they are still to be checked against its lists and those counsel clears (C0.2), and a teacher reads the sentences.
