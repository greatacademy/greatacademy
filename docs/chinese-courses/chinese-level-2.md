# Chinese Level 2

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md). [简体中文](chinese-level-2.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-level-2`
- **Level:** Level 2, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 1](chinese-level-1.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 13 units · 217 topics (30 of characters, 77 of words, 70 of grammar, 24 of listening and speaking, 4 of sentence building, 2 of writing, 10 of handwriting) · 541 knowledge points

## Aim

Level 2 teaches 300 more characters and 770 words of daily life, in twelve themed units from the daily routine and the home to travel, feelings, nature and festivals. Characters and words are learned as in level 1, and every sentence is written only with characters already taught. The characters were chosen to be close to levels 2 and 3 of the standard (GF 0025-2021); they are still to be checked against its lists and those counsel clears (C0.2), and a teacher reads the sentences. Each unit ends with the grammar points its theme uses, after those of the standard: choosing the form that completes a sentence, and the one right sentence among four, every option written with the characters taught by then. Checking the points against the standard's list, and a teacher's reading of the sentences, remain. Every third unit ends with a topic of sentence building, the first writing: putting the parts of a sentence in order, thirty short sentences and thirty longer ones written with the words, grammar and characters of its three units; a teacher's reading of the sentences remains. The sixth and twelfth units end with a topic of paragraph writing as well, judged by a model against three yes-or-no criteria, at least 80 characters a piece, its tasks and model answers written with the characters taught by then; the criteria wait for texts scored by teachers of Chinese, and an admin routes them before they reach learners. After its grammar, each unit has a topic of listening and one of speaking, made from its words and their sentences read by a synthetic voice: the meaning of a word heard, and the word heard in a sentence; then the words said after the recording and the sentences read aloud, heard by a speech service whose thresholds await recordings of real learners. A last unit, Writing Characters, has the learner write by hand 100 characters it already reads, ten a topic, stroke by stroke on the pad in the standard order, first traced and then from memory, judged by the stroke checker against the standard medians; by the end of the level it has written 200, which meets the bands of the handwriting list of GF 0025-2021: 300 by level 3, 700 by level 6 and 1,200 by level 9.
