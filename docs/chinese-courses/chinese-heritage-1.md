# Chinese for Heritage Learners 1

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md). [简体中文](chinese-heritage-1.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-heritage-1`
- **Level:** Literacy bridge 1, for learners who speak Chinese
- **Prerequisite(s):** Speaking Chinese at home
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 4 units · 66 topics (8 of pinyin, 6 of strokes and components, 50 of characters, 2 of their own) · 132 knowledge points

## Aim

The first literacy bridge takes a learner who speaks Chinese but cannot yet read it to about 600 characters: pinyin, the strokes and their order, the common components, the first 600 characters of the first table in words, and the two things a speaker most often writes wrong, characters that sound alike and 的, 地, 得 with the pronouns of the third person. Its characters are the recognition topics of the native courses; passages written for older learners are still to come.
