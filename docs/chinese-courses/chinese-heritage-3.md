# Chinese for Heritage Learners 3

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md). [简体中文](chinese-heritage-3.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-heritage-3`
- **Level:** Literacy bridge 3, for learners who speak Chinese
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese for Heritage Learners 2](chinese-heritage-2.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 2 units · 77 topics (75 of characters, 2 of their own) · 154 knowledge points

## Aim

The third bridge completes the 2,500 characters of the first table with the 900 of grades 3 and 4, tells spoken from written Chinese, and reads traditional characters by their simplified forms. After it, the diagnostic places the learner in the grade course that matches their reading. Its characters are the recognition topics of the native courses; passages written for older learners are still to come.
