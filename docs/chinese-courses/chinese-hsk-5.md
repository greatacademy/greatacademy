# HSK 5

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md) §8.5. [简体中文](chinese-hsk-5.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-hsk-5`, in the group Chinese Exams
- **Level:** Level 5 of the new HSK, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 4](chinese-level-4.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 14 units · 254 topics (10 new, all practice-only) · 644 knowledge points · 4 conversations

## The Test

The new HSK has nine levels built on the *Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for International Chinese Language Education* (GF 0025-2021), which level 5 of this subject follows. In the structure of the papers the test owner published in December 2025, HSK 5 has 72 items in about 110 minutes: listening of 35 in two parts, reading of 35 in three, and writing of 2: a story of at least 100 characters from four pictures, and a text of at least 200. This course practises what each part asks in some form: the word that fills a sentence, the meaning of a word, the character of a word, building sentences, words and sentences heard, repeating and reading aloud, short texts, and the first part of the writing paper. Questions, texts and word lists of the test are never copied: every item here comes from the data of level 5 or is written for this course.

## Aim

The course turns level 5 into test practice. It holds the units of [Chinese Level 5](chinese-level-5.md), so a learner who has not taken it learns it here, and then ten exam topics that mix the words and characters of every theme, as a test does, rather than one batch at a time. It teaches nothing new. Its own topics are practice-only: a lesson starts with practice, and the explanation appears only after a failed attempt. The topics it reuses keep their place in the graph, so a learner who has learned them in the level course is credited and moves on. The units' conversations come with them, 4 in all, spoken in Mandarin with an AI tutor and offered once an admin routes them.

## Units

1 to 13. The units of [Chinese Level 5](chinese-level-5.md), unchanged, its unit of writing characters by hand last.

14. **Exam Practice**

- `hsk-5-gaps` HSK 5: Words in Sentences (practice-only; after the last word topic of level 5). Every sentence of level 5 with its gap: (1) five words to choose from, all of its batch, none of those kept apart from it; (2) six, or five where the batch has no more that could not also fit.
- `hsk-5-meanings` HSK 5: Words and Meanings (practice-only). Every word of level 5: (1) the word of a meaning among four; (2) the meaning of a word among four, in the learner's language.
- `hsk-5-characters` HSK 5: Characters (practice-only). Every character of level 5: (1) by its reading and meaning, among characters that look like it; (2) the character missing from a word of level 5, given the word's pinyin and meaning, among the same, leaving out a word whose meaning would give it away.
- `hsk-5-sentences` HSK 5: Building Sentences (practice-only; after the last sentence-building topic of level 5). The sentences of the sentence-building topics of level 5, mixed, each topic in turn: (1) short ones of three or four parts; (2) longer ones of five to seven.
- `hsk-5-listening` HSK 5: Listening (practice-only; after the last listening topic of level 5). The words and sentences of every unit of level 5, heard without being seen, the units taking turns: (1) the meaning of a word heard, among three; (2) the word heard in a sentence, among three.
- `hsk-5-speaking` HSK 5: Speaking (practice-only; after the last speaking topic of level 5). The same words and sentences, heard by a speech service: (1) a word said after its recording; (2) a sentence read aloud.
- `hsk-5-dialogues` HSK 5: Dialogues and Talks (practice-only; after `hsk-5-listening`). Dialogues between a woman and a man, and talks by one speaker, written for this course in the characters of the levels it tests and heard in two voices without being seen, each with a question of four answers, as the listening paper asks: (1) short dialogues of two lines; (2) longer ones with several questions.
- `hsk-5-passages` HSK 5: Reading Texts (practice-only; after `hsk-5-gaps`). Texts in the same characters, each with a question of four answers, as the reading paper asks: (1) short texts; (2) longer passages with several questions.
- `hsk-5-writing` HSK 5: Writing (practice-only; after the last writing topic of level 5). Short texts written as the writing paper asks, judged by a model: a knowledge point for each kind of paragraph writing of level 5, with its tasks, judged by its three criteria.
- `hsk-5-scene-stories` HSK 5: A Story from Four Scenes (practice-only; after the last paragraph writing of level 5). The first part of the writing paper, where the paper shows four pictures: four scenes described in a line each, told as one story of at least 100 characters, sixteen tasks, judged by a model on three criteria: every scene is told, the scenes are linked as one story, and the language is of the level.

**Not yet practised:** the spoken tasks of the HSKK beyond repeating and reading aloud. The pictures of the story are described in words until the course has pictures of its own.
