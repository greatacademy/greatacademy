# HSK 7–9

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md) §8.5. [简体中文](chinese-hsk-7-9.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-hsk-7-9`, in the group Chinese Exams
- **Level:** Level 7–9 of the new HSK, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 6](chinese-level-6.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 38 units · 985 topics (10 new, all practice-only) · 2655 knowledge points · 12 conversations

## The Test

The new HSK has nine levels built on the *Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for International Chinese Language Education* (GF 0025-2021), which levels 7 to 9 of this subject follow. HSK 7–9 is one test of 98 items in about 210 minutes, in the structure the test owner publishes: listening of 40 in three parts (sentences of news judged true or false, long dialogues and talks with questions); reading of 47 (passages with questions, paragraphs put in order, short answers); writing of 2 (a chart described and analysed in about 200 characters, and an essay of about 600 arguing a position); translation of 4 (two texts translated in writing and two interpreted, into Chinese); and speaking of 5. This course practises what each part asks in some form: the word that fills a sentence, the meaning of a word, the character of a word, building sentences, words and sentences heard, repeating and reading aloud, short texts, the chart, the essay and written translation from English. Questions, texts and word lists of the test are never copied: every item here comes from the data of levels 7 to 9 or is written for this course.

## Aim

The course turns levels 7 to 9 into test practice. It holds the units of [Chinese Level 7](chinese-level-7.md), [Chinese Level 8](chinese-level-8.md), [Chinese Level 9](chinese-level-9.md), so a learner who has not taken them learns them here, and then ten exam topics that mix the words and characters of every theme, as a test does, rather than one batch at a time. It teaches nothing new. Its own topics are practice-only: a lesson starts with practice, and the explanation appears only after a failed attempt. The topics it reuses keep their place in the graph, so a learner who has learned them in the level course is credited and moves on. The units' conversations come with them, 12 in all, spoken in Mandarin with an AI tutor and offered once an admin routes them.

## Units

1 to 36. The units of [Chinese Level 7](chinese-level-7.md), [Chinese Level 8](chinese-level-8.md), [Chinese Level 9](chinese-level-9.md), unchanged.

37. **Writing Characters**: the three levels' units of writing characters by hand, as one unit.

38. **Exam Practice**

- `hsk-7-9-gaps` HSK 7–9: Words in Sentences (practice-only; after the last word topic of levels 7 to 9). Every sentence of levels 7 to 9 with its gap: (1) five words to choose from, all of its batch, none of those kept apart from it; (2) six, or five where the batch has no more that could not also fit.
- `hsk-7-9-meanings` HSK 7–9: Words and Meanings (practice-only). Every word of levels 7 to 9: (1) the word of a meaning among four; (2) the meaning of a word among four, in the learner's language.
- `hsk-7-9-characters` HSK 7–9: Characters (practice-only). Every character of levels 7 to 9: (1) by its reading and meaning, among characters that look like it; (2) the character missing from a word of levels 7 to 9, given the word's pinyin and meaning, among the same, leaving out a word whose meaning would give it away.
- `hsk-7-9-sentences` HSK 7–9: Building Sentences (practice-only; after the last sentence-building topic of levels 7 to 9). The sentences of the sentence-building topics of levels 7 to 9, mixed, each topic in turn: (1) short ones of three or four parts; (2) longer ones of five to seven.
- `hsk-7-9-listening` HSK 7–9: Listening (practice-only; after the last listening topic of level 9). The words and sentences of every unit of levels 7 to 9, heard without being seen, the units taking turns: (1) the meaning of a word heard, among three; (2) the word heard in a sentence, among three.
- `hsk-7-9-speaking` HSK 7–9: Speaking (practice-only; after the last speaking topic of level 9). The same words and sentences, heard by a speech service: (1) a word said after its recording; (2) a sentence read aloud.
- `hsk-7-9-writing` HSK 7–9: Writing (practice-only; after the last writing topic of levels 7 to 9). Short texts written as the writing paper asks, judged by a model: a knowledge point for each kind of paragraph writing of levels 7 to 9, with its tasks, judged by its three criteria.
- `hsk-7-9-chart-writing` HSK 7–9: Describing a Chart (practice-only; after the last writing topic of level 9). The first part of the writing paper, judged by a model: twelve charts given as tables of figures, each described and analysed in about 200 characters, judged on its figures, its analysis and its language.
- `hsk-7-9-essays` HSK 7–9: An Argumentative Essay (practice-only; after the same). The second part: twelve questions, each argued in about 600 characters, judged on a clear position, reasons that support it and answer an opposing view, and organised, correct language.
- `hsk-7-9-translation` HSK 7–9: Translating into Chinese (practice-only; after the same). The written translation: twelve English texts of 80 to 150 words put into Chinese, judged on what is kept, how accurately and how naturally.

**Not yet practised:** the paper's other kinds of listening and reading, such as news, dialogues, talks and passages with questions, paragraphs put in order and short answers, interpreting, and the spoken tasks beyond repeating and reading aloud.
