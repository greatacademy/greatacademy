# Pinyin

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md). [简体中文](chinese-pinyin.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-pinyin`
- **Level:** Before level 1, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** None
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 3 units · 6 topics (6 of pinyin) · 12 knowledge points

## Aim

Pinyin is the tool a learner of Chinese reads sounds with. This course teaches it from print: the four tones and where the mark goes, the 21 initials and the finals, the spelling rules that write i, u and ü as y, w and u and shorten iou, uei and uen, and the tones 一, 不 and two third tones take in speech while pinyin writes their own. Every syllable it asks about is the reading of a character the subject teaches. Hearing tones and syllables, and saying them, join it with the recordings.

## Units

### 1. Tones

- `pinyin-tones` Tones and Tone Marks: Reading the Tone Mark; Placing the Mark

### 2. Initials and Finals

- `pinyin-initials` Initials: Finding the Initial; Choosing by the Initial
- `pinyin-finals` Finals: Finding the Final; Choosing by the Final
- `pinyin-spelling` Spelling Rules: y, w, ü and Shortened Finals: Writing the Syllable; Finding the Final of the Table

### 3. Tones in Speech

- `pinyin-yi-and-bu` The Tones of 一 and 不: Saying 一; Saying 不
- `pinyin-third-tones` Two Third Tones: Saying Them; Writing Them
