# HSK 6

> This is Great Academy's own outline. Its design is in the [Chinese curriculum](../chinese-curriculum.md) §8.5. [简体中文](chinese-hsk-6.zh-CN.md).

- **Course:** `chinese-hsk-6`, in the group Chinese Exams
- **Level:** Level 6 of the new HSK, for learners of Chinese as a foreign language
- **Prerequisite(s):** [Chinese Level 5](chinese-level-5.md)
- **Language of explanation:** The interface language: Chinese or English
- **Size:** 13 units · 243 topics (7 new, all practice-only) · 630 knowledge points · 4 conversations

## The Test

The new HSK has nine levels built on the *Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for International Chinese Language Education* (GF 0025-2021), which level 6 of this subject follow. The question types of each level's papers are still to be confirmed from the test owner's own syllabus and sample papers (C6, a person's step); until then this course practises what every level's reading paper asks in some form: the word that fills a sentence, the meaning of a word, and the character of a word. Questions, texts and word lists of the test are never copied: every item here comes from the data of level 6.

## Aim

The course turns level 6 into test practice. It holds the units of [Chinese Level 6](chinese-level-6.md), so a learner who has not taken it learns it here, and then seven exam topics that mix the words and characters of every theme, as a test does, rather than one batch at a time. It teaches nothing new. Its own topics are practice-only: a lesson starts with practice, and the explanation appears only after a failed attempt. The topics it reuses keep their place in the graph, so a learner who has learned them in the level course is credited and moves on. The units' conversations come with them, 4 in all, spoken in Mandarin with an AI tutor and offered once an admin routes them.

## Units

1 to 12. The units of [Chinese Level 6](chinese-level-6.md), unchanged.

13. **Exam Practice**

- `hsk-6-gaps` HSK 6: Words in Sentences (practice-only; after the last word topic of level 6). Every sentence of level 6 with its gap: (1) five words to choose from, all of its batch, none of those kept apart from it; (2) six, or five where the batch has no more that could not also fit.
- `hsk-6-meanings` HSK 6: Words and Meanings (practice-only). Every word of level 6: (1) the word of a meaning among four; (2) the meaning of a word among four, in the learner's language.
- `hsk-6-characters` HSK 6: Characters (practice-only). Every character of level 6: (1) by its reading and meaning, among characters that look like it; (2) the character missing from a word of level 6, given the word's pinyin and meaning, among the same, leaving out a word whose meaning would give it away.
- `hsk-6-sentences` HSK 6: Building Sentences (practice-only; after the last sentence-building topic of level 6). The sentences of the sentence-building topics of level 6, mixed, each topic in turn: (1) short ones of three or four parts; (2) longer ones of five to seven.
- `hsk-6-listening` HSK 6: Listening (practice-only; after the last listening topic of level 6). The words and sentences of every unit of level 6, heard without being seen, the units taking turns: (1) the meaning of a word heard, among three; (2) the word heard in a sentence, among three.
- `hsk-6-speaking` HSK 6: Speaking (practice-only; after the last speaking topic of level 6). The same words and sentences, heard by a speech service: (1) a word said after its recording; (2) a sentence read aloud.
- `hsk-6-writing` HSK 6: Writing (practice-only; after the last writing topic of level 6). Short texts written as the writing paper asks, judged by a model: a knowledge point for each kind of paragraph writing of level 6, with its tasks, judged by its three criteria.

**Later, once the papers' question types are confirmed:** the other kinds of listening, such as dialogues with questions, and the spoken tasks of the HSKK beyond repeating and reading aloud.
